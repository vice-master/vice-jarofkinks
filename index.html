Rate each one from 1 (a little) to 5 (overflowing). Skip anything that isn't your thing and the jar stays empty. Then save the picture.

Step 1

What's your name?

It goes on the line at the top. A nickname is fine, or leave it blank.

Step 2

Pick your color

This is what the jars get filled with.

Step 3

Fill your jars

Tap a number. Tap the same number again to empty the jar.

Step 4

Build your own list

One item per line. Keep it short — ten to twenty jars reads better than forty. Change the heading too and these jars can be about anything.

Step 5

Share it, locked

This makes a link that carries your list inside it. Whoever opens it fills in their own jars and saves their own picture — they can't edit or see your answers, and the list editor isn't there for them.
